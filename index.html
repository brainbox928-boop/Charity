A simple, thoughtful way to help

Three steps to support a project

Choose a cause that matters to you, follow the clear payment instructions, and send your confirmation for manual review.

  1. 01

    Choose a project

    Explore the initiatives, read what each one aims to support, and select the project you care about.

    Browse projects
  2. 02

    Choose your contribution

    Enter an amount that feels right for you, then choose bank transfer, request the official PayPal link, or select a listed cryptocurrency.

    Continue to contribution details
  3. 03

    Confirm your transfer

    After following the payment instructions, enter your reference and upload the receipt for the team to review manually.

    No automatic card payment
