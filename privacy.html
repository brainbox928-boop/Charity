Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current website and its manual contribution-evidence review process.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Who receives contributions

This website identifies GlobalHope Foundation, located at 9450 Southwest Gemini Drive, Beaverton, OR 97008, USA, as the intended recipient of contribution enquiries and evidence submissions.

Information you provide

The checkout does not process or collect a payment. When you submit payment evidence, the form sends your name, email, contribution amount and currency, selected project and payment method, payment reference, optional note, preferences, and uploaded image or PDF to Netlify Forms for manual review. The submission and file are accessible to authorized site administrators. Netlify's privacy and security terms also apply.

We retain evidence submissions for no longer than 12 months after receipt, then delete them from the Netlify Forms dashboard unless a legal requirement or active dispute requires longer retention. Deletion is performed by an administrator and is not automated; Netlify may retain backups under its own policies. Upload only the evidence needed to identify your transfer. Hide unrelated balances, full card numbers, passwords, security codes, and other sensitive information.

Monthly-gift interest does not start a recurring payment. We use contact details only to review a contribution, provide an acknowledgment if requested, respond to your enquiry, or contact you when clarification is necessary. We do not add supporters to a fundraising mailing list or send repeated donation requests. Public listing consent is optional and recorded for staff review; supporter details are never published automatically. An acknowledgment is not a tax receipt, and no tax deduction or benefit is promised.

Contact form and newsletter

If you use the contact form, your name, email, topic, and message are sent to Netlify Forms and used only to reply to you. If you subscribe to project updates, your email address is stored in Netlify Forms and used only to send occasional project updates; you can unsubscribe at any time by emailing us. Subscribing is optional and separate from making a contribution. If you add a tribute (in honor or memory of someone) to a contribution, the name you enter is stored with your submission.

Third-party services and technical data

The site loads fonts, Bootstrap assets, and icons from third-party services. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when the assets load. No project photos or analytics scripts are currently configured. If you add externally hosted media or other services, update this policy to explain what information those providers may receive.

How to ask a question or request a change

For privacy questions or requests concerning a submission, contact globalhopeonline@gmail.com, call +1 681 505 6975, or write to GlobalHope Foundation, 9450 Southwest Gemini Drive, Beaverton, OR 97008, USA.

Updates

This policy should be revised whenever the website changes how Netlify Forms, uploaded files, payment providers, donor data, analytics, mailing lists, or other personal information are handled.
