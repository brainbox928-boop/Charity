Contribution Terms

These plain-language terms describe the current manual checkout and are subject to applicable local laws.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Recipient and purpose

This website identifies GlobalHope Foundation as the intended recipient and invites external support for the listed community initiatives around the world.

Manual payment only

This website does not process card payments, initiate bank transfers, or verify transactions. The checkout can submit payment evidence through Netlify Forms for manual review. Submitting a receipt or screenshot confirms only that evidence was received; it does not confirm that a payment was verified.

Before sending money, independently verify that payment instructions and account or wallet details belong to the intended recipient. Do not send funds based only on an unverified message or page.

External support and project needs

GlobalHope is seeking contributions to help develop and support its listed initiatives. Contributions are appreciated, but this website does not guarantee a specific project outcome or state that a project is already funded. Add confirmed project budgets, allocation details, and any restrictions before accepting contributions.

Monthly gifts and receipts

Selecting monthly-gift interest does not authorize or schedule a recurring charge. The current website cannot collect recurring-payment instructions. A receipt request is not an automated receipt and does not establish that a contribution is tax deductible. Tax treatment depends on the recipient’s legal status and the donor’s local rules.

Refunds and questions

This draft does not set a refund guarantee or cancellation process. Contact contributions@globalhope.org to ask about a contribution. The organization must add its approved refund and dispute policy here before launch.
