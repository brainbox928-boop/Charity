Privacy Policy

This policy describes the current website and its manual contribution-evidence review process.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

Who receives contributions

This website identifies GlobalHope Foundation as the intended recipient of contribution enquiries and evidence submissions.

Information you provide

The checkout does not process or collect a payment. When you submit payment evidence, the form sends your name, email, contribution amount and currency, selected project and payment method, payment reference, optional note, preferences, and uploaded image or PDF to Netlify Forms for manual review. The submission and file are stored in the GlobalHope site's Netlify account and are accessible to authorized site administrators. Netlify's own privacy, security, and retention terms also apply.

Upload only the evidence needed to identify your transfer. Hide unrelated balances, full card numbers, passwords, security codes, and other sensitive information. Monthly-gift interest does not start a recurring payment; receipt requests and public listing consent are recorded for staff review only. This site does not automatically issue receipts or publish donor information.

Third-party services and technical data

The site loads fonts, Bootstrap assets, and icons from third-party services. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details when the assets load. No project photos or analytics scripts are currently configured. If you add externally hosted media or other services, update this policy to explain what information those providers may receive.

How to ask a question or request a change

For privacy questions or requests concerning a submission, contact contributions@globalhope.org. The organization must define its response process and retention period for form submissions and uploaded evidence.

Updates

This policy should be revised whenever the website changes how Netlify Forms, uploaded files, payment providers, donor data, analytics, mailing lists, or other personal information are handled.
