Recipient and purpose

This website identifies GlobalHope Foundation, located at 9450 Southwest Gemini Drive, Beaverton, OR 97008, USA, as the intended recipient and invites external support for the listed community initiatives around the world.

Manual payment only

This website does not process card payments, initiate bank transfers, or verify transactions. The checkout can submit payment evidence through Netlify Forms for manual review. Submitting a receipt or screenshot confirms only that evidence was received; it does not confirm that a payment was verified.

Before sending money, independently verify that payment instructions and account or wallet details belong to the intended recipient. Do not send funds based only on an unverified message or page.

External support and project needs

GlobalHope is seeking contributions to help develop and support its listed initiatives. Contributions are appreciated, but this website does not guarantee a specific project outcome or state that a project is already funded. Add confirmed project budgets, allocation details, and any restrictions before accepting contributions.

Contact preferences and receipts

Selecting monthly-gift interest does not authorize or schedule a recurring charge. We will reply with monthly-giving information only when you request it. We do not add supporters to a fundraising mailing list or send repeated donation requests. A contribution acknowledgment is not a tax receipt. GlobalHope does not promise tax deductibility or tax benefits; consult a qualified tax adviser about your circumstances.

Refund requests

Contact us within 30 days of a contribution if it was duplicated or made by mistake. We will acknowledge the request within five business days, review it, and explain the decision. Approved refunds will be returned through the original payment method where possible and where permitted by law. Transfers already committed to project costs may not be recoverable. Cryptocurrency transfers are generally irreversible; any refund request will be reviewed individually, and network fees may not be recoverable.
